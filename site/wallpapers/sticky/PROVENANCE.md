# The Sticky pack

Sixteen 480x800 one-bit wallpapers drawn for the Seeed reTerminal Sticky's panel.

Every one is generated by `tools_local/sticky_wallpapers/gen.py` from arithmetic and
fixed seeds. There is no photograph, scan or other artwork in them, so there is
nothing to credit and nothing to license beyond this repository's own terms.

`sticky.dat` is the sixteen files end to end, in the order of `kSticky` in
`src/apps_local/wallpapers/WallpapersCore.cpp`. The Wallpapers app fetches it
from this folder on the `xteink` branch and unpacks it into `/wallpapers`.

To rebuild: `python3 tools_local/sticky_wallpapers/gen.py site/wallpapers/sticky`
